Assumptions to replace

These are placeholders chosen to make the arithmetic work, not measurements. Swap each for a real figure before this goes in front of anyone — the target moves when they change.

  • Average selling price, KES 2,800. Replace with the actual average across your listed catalogue, weighted by what sells.
  • Commission at 12%. Jumia's rate varies by category. Take the real rate from your seller agreement, per category if it differs.
  • Fulfilment at KES 200 per order. Replace with your own packaging, drop-off and returns cost per order.
  • Payment processing at 1.5%. Confirm against a settled statement rather than assuming.
  • The order ramp of 8 → 15 → 24 → 34. This is the load-bearing assumption and nothing on this page supports it yet. Ground it in something real: current listing views, conversion rate, ad spend you have committed, or stock you can actually ship.
  • Returns and cancellations: not modeled at all. Add a line for them — on marketplace apparel and electronics it is rarely under 10%.

How to verify

Hand this to an investor alongside the source. The point of the page is that the baseline is checkable.

  1. Sign in at vendorcenter.jumia.com and open Account Statements.
  2. Read the Due & Unpaid figure at the top. It should match the KES 0.00 shown in “Verified position today”.
  3. Use Export Transactions for the full ledger, including the 24–30 Aug statement not legible in the capture.
  4. Compare any claim on this page against that export. If a number here is not in the export, it is modeled — that is what the rust type and dotted underline mean.
